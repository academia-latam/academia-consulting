FIG. 00 · MANIFESTO

We don't add AI.
We amplify the teacher.

Five convictions about artificial intelligence in the classroom. What we believe, and why we always start with people.

Scroll to read ↓
01 · PRINCIPLE

Class first,
tool second.

The question isn't which app to buy. It's what this classroom needs to learn. Pedagogy leads; a tool comes in only when it earns its place, and almost none do.

02 · PRINCIPLE

Neither ban it,
nor worship it.

Fear and hype end up in the same place: no one learns to use it with judgment. Not banning it as a threat, not worshipping it as a miracle. We teach how to use it well.

03 · PRINCIPLE

Understand the machine,
don't revere it.

AI doesn't think: it predicts. An engine, not an oracle. Once a teacher understands how it works inside, the fear goes and judgment begins.

04 · PRINCIPLE

AI drafts;
the teacher decides.

The machine suggests; the relationship confirms. It can write a first attempt, never watch a student go quiet and know why. Trust, judgment and care don't automate.

05 · PRINCIPLE

Trust,
not control.

Accompanying isn't surveilling. A dashboard that only flags doesn't accompany, and tracking a student isn't caring for them. We protect their data as part of the work, not as a checkbox.
